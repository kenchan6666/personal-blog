## What to build

Visitors submit Comments with display name + email on Published Journals/Articles; Comments stay hidden until Owner approval; Owner can reply as Owner. Projects have no Comments.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Visitor can submit a Comment with name + email on Journal/Article
- [ ] Commenter email is not shown publicly
- [ ] Pending Comments are invisible until Owner approves
- [ ] Owner can approve/reject and reply as Owner
- [ ] Project pages do not accept Comments
